Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

What happens at a murder mystery party?
A host-led interactive game where a fictional murder takes place and all guests are suspects. Through character cards, tasks, and clues, everyone mingles and investigates to identify the murderer.
How does the gameplay work?
Each guest receives a character card describing their persona and giving them "tasks" — instructions to talk to specific other guests, share information, or find clues. Everyone gathers clues throughout the evening.
What type of events work best?
Birthday parties, corporate team-building, holiday parties, family reunions, bachelorette parties, neighborhood gatherings — anything where you want people interacting.
How many guests do I need?
Our events work best with 20 to 40 guests. Every character is unique and every guest has a role in solving the mystery.
Who runs the event?
Our professional party host leads everything from start to finish. They set the scene, introduce characters, guide the investigation, and keep the energy high.
